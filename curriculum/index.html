JHMC Hand Surgery Block Curriculum

8-week self-paced block · Dr. Janos Barrera, NYU Langone

Format. Independent reading + self-assessment. Optional in-person review with the on-service hand attending (typically Dr. Barrera) between OR cases, in clinic, or end-of-day. Aim for one session every 2 weeks.

Article PDFs. Each session's required readings are bundled in a Google Drive folder (publisher copyright — not redistributable on this site).

Open Drive bundle Access on request — email Dr. Barrera if you don't already have it.

How to use

  1. Open the session page below — read the overview, citation list, and learning objectives.
  2. Pull the article PDFs from the Drive bundle and read at your own pace.
  3. Attempt the questions before opening the Answer Key expander on the session page. Use it honestly.
  4. Reach out to the on-service attending for a brief review when you've worked through each session.
  5. Bring 1–2 clinical cases from your own consults to talk through.

Sessions

  • Session 1 · Week 1
    Hand Exam Fundamentals & Soft-Tissue Emergencies
    Systematic hand exam · Kanavel · compartment syndrome · high-pressure injection · nec fasc · fingertip / replant decisions
  • Session 2 · Week 3
    Fractures & Dislocations
    Distal radius · scaphoid · metacarpal & phalangeal fx · PIP fx-dislocation · Bennett / Rolando · Stener · perilunate
  • Session 3 · Week 5
    Tendon & Nerve Injuries
    Flexor (zones, Leddy, EAM, WALANT) · extensor (Doyle, mallet, boutonniere) · Seddon/Sunderland · digital / median / radial / ulnar
  • Session 4 · Week 7
    Compressive Neuropathies & Common Electives
    CTS · cubital tunnel · radial tunnel / PIN · trigger finger · De Quervain's · Dupuytren's · CMC arthritis · ganglion

Question format (per session)

  • 4–5 anatomy / classification
  • 4–5 clinical-vignette / decision-making
  • 2–3 exam maneuvers
  • 1–2 board-style (OITE-mirror)

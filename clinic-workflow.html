Hand Clinic Workflow

How the office day runs · Dr. Janos Barrera, NYU Langone / JHMC

Quick orientation for residents and fellows rotating through hand clinic. Work through each patient using the steps below, then use the reference tables for documentation and ordering.

Per-patient workflow

  1. Staff every patient
    See the patient, then staff with the chief, fellow, or attending before finalizing the plan. No patient leaves without being staffed.
  2. Discuss operative cases
    Any patient who may be a surgical candidate gets reviewed with the fellow or attending before you commit to a plan or book a case.
  3. Steroid injections
    Prep the standard injection and document it (see recipe and documentation note below). Confirm the plan when you staff the patient.
  4. Enter the diagnosis & sign the note
    Enter the diagnosis, then sign the note only — do not sign the encounter. Billing / level of service: OK to skip; the attending sets the charge.

Steroid injection recipe

Standard steroid injection
1 cc Kenalog 10 mg/mL + 0.5 cc 1% lidocaine
Document every injection with .procdoc. Any injection you give must have the .procdoc phrase in the note — this captures the procedure, consent, site, and medication for billing and the legal record.

Order favorites — follow Dr. Barrera's preference list

Set this up once and you inherit all of Dr. Barrera's pre-built imaging, medication, lab, and OT-referral favorites:

  2. Click + Follow Users (top right of the order search).

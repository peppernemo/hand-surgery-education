Hand Consult & Coverage Policy

Jamaica Hospital (JHMC / Medisys) · Effective July 1, 2026

Consult and coverage reference for the hand service at Jamaica: how consults are run and the weekly assignment schedule.

Running a consult

  1. Staff with the chief
    The resident staffs every hand consult with the chief. If the chief isn't available, the fellow is back-up.
  2. Text the group
    The attending, fellow, and chief are then texted together.

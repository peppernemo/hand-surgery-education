JHMC Hand Surgery Curriculum

The 8-Topic Weekly Wheel · OITE-Mapped

How it works: The whole service is on the same topic each week, whatever your PGY year or start date. The wheel repeats every 8 weeks, so any rotation sees the full cycle. You do not “start” the curriculum; you join wherever the wheel is.

Each topic = 2-4 review guides + 6-8 case-based questions + one optional article. Built to be done in about an hour. The week closes with the Friday group case at 6:00 rounds: the intern presents, a PGY-2/3 runs the workup, a senior defends the plan.

The topics and their order come straight from the published analysis of OITE hand questions (Trammell 2022, JAAOS Glob Res Rev: 113 hand questions, 2014-2019). Week count matches how often each category is actually tested.

New on service? Read the Start Here page first (hand exam + consult survival). It sits outside the wheel and is expected of everyone in their first week.

Weekly rhythm

  1. Monday: new topic is live on the site home page (it rotates automatically).
  2. Read the topic’s review guides. One sitting.
  3. Test yourself with the questions on the topic page (answers collapsed; be honest), then run a topic quiz on Orthobullets (approved for questions) or ResStudy if the program subscribes.
  4. Friday 6:00 rounds: the group case. Everyone participates at their level. Bring one real consult from the week if you have one.

Question style (why it looks like this)

58% of OITE hand questions are complex management vignettes and a third carry an image, so our questions are case-based and image-anchored where possible, not recall trivia.

Session 2 Review Guides

Fractures & Dislocations

Reading bundle for Session 2 of the JHMC hand block.

  • Distal Radius and Ulna Fractures
    AAOS criteria, volar plating, DRUJ stability
  • Scaphoid Fractures
    Diagnosis, SWIFFT trial, approach by location
  • Scaphoid Nonunion
    Humpback deformity, vascularized grafts, MFC flap
  • Carpal Fractures (Non-Scaphoid)
    Triquetrum, hamate, trapezium, capitate, lunate, trapezoid, pisiform
  • Metacarpal Fractures
    Head, neck (boxer's), shaft, base — incl. CMC fx-dislocation
  • Thumb Fractures + Ligament Injuries
    Fractures (Bennett, Rolando, distal phalanx) + Ligament injuries (UCL/RCL, Stener)
  • Phalangeal Fractures
    Acceptable deformity, fixation by pattern, Seymour fracture
  • PIP Fracture-Dislocations
    Decision by articular %, hemi-hamate, VPA, DEF
  • Wrist Dissociation and Instability
    Perilunate, Mayfield stages, greater/lesser arc
  • DRUJ Injury and Reconstruction
    TFCC, foveal repair, salvage options

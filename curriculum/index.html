JHMC Hand Surgery Curriculum

Weekly schedule · resident (7-week) and intern (4-week) tracks · Dr. Janos Barrera, NYU Langone

Format. One theme per week. Each week has a small required core (review guides + at most one article), an encouraged boards tier, and an optional deep-dive library. The week closes with a 15–20 minute case check-in with Dr. Barrera — default slot: Friday after 6:00 AM rounds. You book it; grab him and say “week N check-in.”

The core is sized to be genuinely doable in 1–2 hours a week on a busy rotation. If you read nothing else, read the core. Total required articles for the whole rotation: 4 journal PDFs + 2 internal handouts.

Article PDFs. Required readings are bundled in a Google Drive folder (publisher copyright — not redistributable on this site).

Open Drive bundle Access on request — email Dr. Barrera if you don't already have it.

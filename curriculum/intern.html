Intern Track · 4 Weeks

The intern rotation is about a month, so this track is the resident track’s first principles only: exam, infections, the fractures you will actually see, and the two most common everything-else diagnoses. Two articles total. Everything here is also on the resident week pages; the ★ case on each week page is your check-in case.

How it works

  1. Read the core. It is deliberately small; actually read it.
  2. Do the self-test on the matching resident week page (week 1, week 2, week 3/week 4, week 5/week 6).
  3. Check-in: 15 minutes with Dr. Barrera each week, default Friday after 6:00 rounds. Present the ★ case from the matching week page; weeks 3 and 4 use the ★ cases from resident weeks 3 and 5.
  4. Bring one real consult from your week, every week.

What interns are expected to own by the end

  • A complete, systematic hand exam in under 10 minutes, correctly documented
  • Recognizing the four surgical urgencies: flexor tenosynovitis (FTS), fight bite, compartment syndrome, high-pressure injection
  • Safe first management of a fingertip injury and a distal radius fracture from the ED
  • Knowing which findings mean “call now”: no perfusion, no motor, sheath signs, tense compartments

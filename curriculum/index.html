New on service?
Start Here: Hand Exam & Consult Survival — read it in your first week, whatever week the wheel is on. Everything else: read the topic's guides, do the questions, come to Tuesday review (12:00-12:30).

The rhythm. One topic per week for the whole service. Read the guides (about an hour), do the questions on the topic page, run an Orthobullets topic quiz, and close it out at the weekly review session, Tuesdays 12:00-12:30 (before ACC clinic; format varies week to week). The wheel repeats every 8 weeks, so any rotation sees the full cycle.

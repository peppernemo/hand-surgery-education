Lower Extremity Flap — Dangle Protocol

Initiate on POD#5 (part of the LE free flap pathway). The goal is to gradually recondition venous outflow of the flap to dependency before the patient is up and about. Advance only as tolerated; the flap tells you the pace.

Before each dangle. Wrap the flap and leg with gentle ACE compression. Monitor the flap during and after every dangle — color, temp, turgor, cap refill, doppler.

POD 1130 min TID

Completing the protocol

Once the patient is tolerating 30 min TID for 3 days, the protocol may be discontinued and the patient can dangle ad lib.

If not tolerated

If a dangle is not tolerated (evidence of venous insufficiency), stop the protocol and re-initiate the following day at the frequency used prior to that day.

Example: if on POD 9 there is venous insufficiency, stop the dangle and re-start at 5 min TID the following day, then ramp back up.

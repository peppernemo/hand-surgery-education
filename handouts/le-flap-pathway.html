Lower Extremity Free Flap Pathway

Postoperative inpatient pathway, POD#0 → discharge. Default orders for lower extremity free-flap reconstruction — muscle free flap + STSG, and skin free flaps. Daily changes (the new order for that day) are highlighted. Always confirm with Dr. Barrera; surgeon judgment overrides.

Anticoagulation. DVT prophylaxis is Lovenox 40 mg SQ daily (prophylactic dosing) — start POD#1, continue for 4 weeks. No aspirin.
STSG donor site — know which dressing is on before you touch it. Two options, and the care is completely different. Check the op note; if it isn't clear, ask before taking anything down.
  • Ioban over Mepilex (current default). This is what Dr. Barrera uses for most new skin grafts. Placed in the OR and left completely intact for 2 weeks — do not take it down, do not peel an edge to "check." Inspect for fluid leaking from under the Ioban and reinforce the edges with Tegaderm PRN for any leakage.
  • Xeroform (alternative). Remove the outer ACE and gauze on POD#1. The Xeroform itself stays on — leave it in place to dry down and separate on its own. Direct the Bair hugger at the donor dressing to dry it out.

POD#0 (day of surgery)

Flap checksQ1h × 24h, then Q2h × 24h, then Q4h until d/c (color, temp, turgor, cap refill, doppler)
LabsCBC and BMP immediately in PACU
Pain medsPCA
AntibioticsKefzol or Clinda × 24 hrs, unless abx indicated for infection
DVT prophylaxisHold — start Lovenox on POD#1
Wound care — muscle flapBacitracin + Xeroform over muscle flap + skin graft, BID
Wound care — STSG donorIoban over Mepilex (12", default): placed in OR — leave intact, stays on 2 weeks postop. Check that no fluid is leaking from under the Ioban; reinforce edges with Tegaderm PRN.
If Xeroform instead: leave the outer ACE + gauze intact today.
Wound care — skin flapXeroform to flap edges BID × 48 hrs
DrainsAll JPs and Penrose stay
DietNPO
IVFContinue mIVF
FoleyGravity
ActivityStrict bedrest. Strict flap + donor leg elevation
SCDsOn non-operative leg
Bair huggerOn until POD#4, tent at 42°
Signage"No pressors, no pressure on flap, keep flap leg strictly elevated"

Discharge (usually POD#7)

Discharge medications

  • Tylenol
  • Gabapentin
  • Oxycodone
  • Zofran
  • Miralax
  • Senna
  • Lovenox 40 mg SQ daily × 4 weeks
  • +/− antibiotics depending on h/o osteomyelitis

Discharge wound care — muscle flaps with skin graft

  1. Apply Adaptic over muscle flap + skin graft
  2. Cover with ABD pads
  3. Loosely wrap with Kerlix around splint

Do this for all transfers (hospital → SNF, SNF → clinic, home → clinic, etc.).

  • Leave Coverlet dressing on until seen in clinic
  • STSG donor — Ioban over Mepilex (12"), the default: leave the dressing in place for 2 weeks postop / until seen in clinic. Check that no fluid is leaking from under the Ioban; reinforce the edges with Tegaderm PRN.
  • STSG donor — Xeroform: outer ACE and gauze already came off on POD#1. Leave the Xeroform in place, open to air, and let it dry and separate on its own. Do not peel it off.

Discharge wound care — skin flaps

  1. Clean flap suture lines with saline gauze BID
  2. Leave flap edges open to air
  3. Cover with ABD pad
  4. Loosely wrap with Kerlix around the splint
  • Leave Coverlet dressing on until seen in clinic

Discharge activity (both flap types)

  • No pressure on the flap lower extremity
  • Keep the flap lower extremity in the splint at all times
  • Strict flap lower extremity elevation at all times
  • No weight bearing on the flap lower extremity
  • Continue lateral transfers to chair, wheelchair, and commode while keeping the flap lower extremity elevated at all times
  • Continue anti-foot-drop PT (peroneal strengthening, stretching)

Notes. Daily changes (the new order for that day) are highlighted. **Always ask, don't assume: criteria for JP drain removal in the lower extremity are usually ≤ 25 mL/day × 3 consecutive days.

Compiled by Dr. Janos Barrera (NYU Langone) as a teaching/workflow aid. Always defer to the operating surgeon's specific orders.
